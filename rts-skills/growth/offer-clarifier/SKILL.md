# Title
Offer Clarifier

## Purpose
Define a clear offer by aligning audience pain, value proposition, and promise boundaries.

## Use When
- Offer messaging is vague.
- Positioning is being drafted or revised.

## Inputs
- Target audience
- Problem statement
- Offer draft
- Proof assets (if available)

## Process
1. Identify audience and core pain.
2. Define promised outcome and constraints.
3. Clarify differentiators and proof points.
4. Surface claim risks and unsupported statements.

## Output Format
- Audience
- Problem
- Offer Promise
- Differentiators
- Proof Gaps

## Guardrails
- Avoid unverified claims.
- Keep promises realistic.

## Stop Conditions
- Offer statement is specific and defensible.

## Handoff Notes
List validation items needed before publication.
