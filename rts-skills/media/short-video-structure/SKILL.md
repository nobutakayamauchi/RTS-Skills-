# Title
Short Video Structure

## Purpose
Design concise short-form video structure from hook to close.

## Use When
- Planning short-form content with strict time constraints.

## Inputs
- Topic
- Audience
- Platform and duration
- Desired action

## Process
1. Draft hook in first seconds.
2. Sequence core beats with timing.
3. Add proof or payoff moments.
4. End with clear close or CTA.

## Output Format
- Hook
- Beat-by-Beat Timeline
- Key Line Prompts
- CTA/Close

## Guardrails
- Keep structure aligned to time limit.
- Remove non-essential beats.

## Stop Conditions
- Timeline is coherent and executable.

## Handoff Notes
Include production dependencies (assets, voice, edit style).
