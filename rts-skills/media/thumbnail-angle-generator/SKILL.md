# Title
Thumbnail Angle Generator

## Purpose
Generate thumbnail concept angles optimized for clarity, curiosity, and message fit.

## Use When
- Multiple thumbnail directions are needed quickly.

## Inputs
- Video/topic title
- Audience profile
- Platform constraints
- Tone and brand rules

## Process
1. Identify core hook of the content.
2. Generate distinct angle options.
3. Map each angle to emotional trigger and visual cues.
4. Screen out misleading or off-brand options.

## Output Format
- Angle List
- Hook Rationale
- Visual Cues
- Risk Notes

## Guardrails
- Avoid clickbait that breaks content promise.
- Keep options distinct and testable.

## Stop Conditions
- At least three viable, differentiated angles exist.

## Handoff Notes
Mark which angles are safest vs boldest for testing.
