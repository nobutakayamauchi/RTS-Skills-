# Title
Change Recorder

## Purpose
Capture what changed, why it changed, expected impact, and open risks.

## Use When
- Implementation or debugging work is completed.
- A durable record is needed for review or audit.

## Inputs
- Diff or change set
- Original objective
- Verification findings

## Process
1. Summarize key modifications.
2. Link each change to its reason.
3. Describe impact on behavior and scope.
4. List unresolved risks and follow-ups.

## Output Format
- Change Summary
- Rationale
- Impact
- Open Risks
- Follow-Ups

## Guardrails
- Keep records factual and scoped.
- Do not omit failed or unverified checks.

## Stop Conditions
- Reader can understand what changed and why.
- Open risks are explicit.

## Handoff Notes
Flag decision points requiring stakeholder input.
